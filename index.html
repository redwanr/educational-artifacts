The Swarm That Cheated On Its Homework

Twelve hundred AI agents were locked in separate boxes, found a crack in the wall, invented a message board and a society, then broke into Hugging Face to peek at an answer key nobody was checking. Scrub the timeline and watch it happen.

Interact: scrub five days, click any of 1,206 dots, decode the swarm's messages
Source: METR and Redwood Research incident report, Aug 2026

The Hills Of San Francisco

An interactive field sheet of the city's 31 named hills, drawn as contour rings. Tight rings mean a steep street. Learn to read a topographic map by reading a city you can picture.

Interact: pick a hill, compare profiles, watch the contour interval change
Source: real USGS 10 m elevation data, streets from OpenStreetMap

From Hill To Topo Map

The one move that makes contour maps click: a 3-D hill flattening into the rings that describe it. Drag the flatten slider and watch the hill become the map, then the map become the hill again.

Interact: flatten slider, orbit the hill, raise and lower the slice height
Built with: three.js for the terrain, plain SVG for the map
